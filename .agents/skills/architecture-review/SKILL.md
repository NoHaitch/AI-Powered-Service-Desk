# Architecture review

1. Compare the proposal with the [project constraints](../../PROJECT_CONTEXT.md), requirements, and applicable ADRs. Map ownership, consumers, rollout and migration, failure behavior, and local operation.
2. Compare credible alternatives on correctness, maintainability, complexity, security, and operating costs. Explain what evidence would change the decision. Bring unresolved public contracts, security boundaries, and spending decisions to the developer.
3. Document the agreed rationale and consequences as an ADR when warranted. Follow the [documentation index](../../../docs/README.md) for its structure.
