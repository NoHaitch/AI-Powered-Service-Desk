title: Fix audio in an online meeting
updated: 2026-11-27
category: Software
summary: Select the right microphone and speakers and run a test call.
---

## Test your audio

1. Check the mute control on both your headset and the meeting app.
2. Select the intended microphone and speaker in the meeting settings.
3. Use the app's audio test or join a test call. If the app runs in a browser, check microphone permission for that site.

## If audio still fails

Check whether the headset works in another app. Tell support whether you could hear others, whether they could hear you, and which devices the meeting app had selected.
