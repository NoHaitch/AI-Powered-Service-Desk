## Narrow down the problem

1. Check that you joined the approved office network.
2. Note whether the disconnects happen at your desk, in a meeting room, or while moving between floors.
3. Ask a coworker nearby whether they are experiencing the same problem.
4. If available, try a wired connection to continue working while support investigates.

## Report the pattern

Provide the building, floor, network name, and approximate times of the disconnects. Mention the services affected and any workaround that helped.
