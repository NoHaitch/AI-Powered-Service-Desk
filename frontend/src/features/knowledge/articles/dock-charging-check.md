## Start with power and connections

Save your work while the laptop still has power. Confirm that the dock's power adapter is firmly connected to the wall and dock. Use the approved adapter for your equipment, since adapters that fit can still have different power ratings.

### Compare charging paths

1. Test the laptop with its approved charger connected directly.
2. Reconnect the powered dock using the cable that came with it or an approved replacement.
3. Close power-heavy applications and check whether the battery indicator shows stable charging.

| Observation | Useful comparison | Detail to capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct charger works | Try an approved alternate dock or cable | Dock and cable models |
| Direct charger fails too | Try a known working outlet with the same charger | Charger and laptop models |
| Charging starts and stops | Note the battery level and connected peripherals | Time and frequency |

## Stop if equipment looks damaged

Leave damaged cables, a swollen battery, or equipment that smells burnt disconnected if you can do so safely. Report the condition to the service desk so it can arrange inspection or replacement.

### Send useful details

Include the equipment asset tags, the comparisons you tried, and whether charging fails only when another accessory is connected.
