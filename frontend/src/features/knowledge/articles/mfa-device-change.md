title: Replace your work MFA device
updated: 2026-11-27
category: Accounts & access
summary: Prepare for a phone change and get help when the old device is unavailable.
---

## If you still have your old phone

Use your organization's approved account security page to enroll and test the replacement. Keep the old device available until you have confirmed the new method works.

## If the phone is lost or inaccessible

Contact your official service desk through a known channel for identity verification and recovery. Say whether the device was lost, stolen, or replaced.

Keep passwords, verification codes, and recovery codes private. If you receive an approval prompt you did not initiate, deny it and report it through your official security channel.
