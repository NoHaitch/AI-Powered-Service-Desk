## Confirm the business need

Identify the workspace owner and which files or channels the guest needs. Agree on whether the guest needs to view or edit content and how long they will need access. Follow your organization's guest-access process and confirm that the workspace can be shared externally.

### Choose the narrowest scope

| Work to complete | Access to request | Owner check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review a single document | View access to that document | Verify it contains only approved material |
| Coauthor project files | Edit access to the relevant folder | Review who can invite further guests |
| Join project discussions | Access to the designated project space | Confirm the scope excludes unrelated channels |

## Submit and verify

1. Provide the guest's work email, business reason, workspace location, and an end date.
2. Have the owner review the request before sending any invitation.
3. Ask the guest to confirm access using the intended work account.
4. Review and remove access when the project ends under your organization's process.

### If the invitation fails

Record the invitation time and error, and check that the guest used the address named in the invitation. Share the minimum necessary information with support.
