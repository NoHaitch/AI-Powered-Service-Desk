## Identify where sharing is blocked

Try the Share control in a test meeting and read any message that appears. Some meetings let only the organizer or presenter share. If the organizer allows sharing and the app still shows an error, check whether the app or browser has the required local permission.

### Compare common symptoms

| What you see | Where to check | Safe test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share button is unavailable | Meeting role and organizer settings | Ask the organizer to make you a presenter |
| Permission prompt appears | Browser or device privacy settings | Allow the approved meeting app and retry |
| Participants see a blank area | Selection of the shared window or tab | Share a non-sensitive test window |

## Verify after changing a setting

1. End any active share and close confidential windows.
2. Reopen the approved meeting app if it requests a restart.
3. Join a test call and share a single window.
4. Ask the other participant to confirm what they see.

### Report persistent issues

Note the meeting app, device type, and exact message. Include whether the problem affects all meetings or just meetings hosted by someone else.
