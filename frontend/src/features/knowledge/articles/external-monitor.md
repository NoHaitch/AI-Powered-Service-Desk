updated: 2026-11-27

---

## Check the display

1. Confirm the monitor has power and select the input that matches the connected cable.
2. Reconnect the video cable at both ends and check that the dock is powered.
3. If your equipment supports it, connect the display directly to the laptop to see whether the dock is involved.
4. Open your device's display settings and check whether it detects the monitor.

## What to tell support

Include the monitor and dock models, the type of cable, and whether the monitor works with another device. Describe whether the display is blank, flickering, or showing an input error.
