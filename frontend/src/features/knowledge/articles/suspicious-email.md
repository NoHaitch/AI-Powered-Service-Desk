---
title: Report a suspicious email
updated: 2026-11-27
category: Security
summary: Use the approved reporting channel and tell security what happened.
---

## If you have not interacted with the message

- Leave any links and attachments unopened.
- Use your organization's approved phishing reporting feature or contact its security team through a known channel.
- Include the message subject and the time you received it, following your organization's reporting procedure.

## If you already interacted with it

Contact the security team promptly. Explain whether you opened an attachment, followed a link, or entered information, and provide the approximate time. Use the team's instructions for any follow-up steps.
