## Check whether the device is eligible

Use your organization's mobile-device policy to confirm supported devices, operating-system versions, and whether personal devices are allowed. Start only from the approved enrollment instructions. Review the prompts so you understand what configuration or access the organization requests.

### Gather the information you need

| Device situation | What to prepare | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company-issued phone | Work account and device asset tag | The phone is assigned to you |
| Approved personal phone | Work account and the required OS version | You consent to the work profile policy |
| Replacement device | Access to your current sign-in method | The old device is available until setup is verified |

## Verify the work profile

1. Follow the approved enrollment steps over a reliable connection.
2. Open the designated work app and sign in with your work account.
3. Check that email or another expected work service is available.
4. Confirm that your approved verification method still works before retiring an old device.

### If enrollment fails

Record the step, the exact error, the OS version, and whether the device was enrolled before. Share device identifiers only through an approved support channel.
