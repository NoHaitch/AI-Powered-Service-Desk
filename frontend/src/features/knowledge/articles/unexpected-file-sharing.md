---
title: Respond to an unexpected file-sharing notification
updated: 2026-11-27
category: Security
summary: Verify unexpected invitations through known channels and report suspicious ones.
---

## Verify the context

Consider whether you expected a file from the named sender and whether the message fits an active project. If you need to ask the sender, start a new message through a known contact or use your normal collaboration app. Avoid using the unexpected notification itself to contact them.

### Compare signals

| Signal | What to verify | Safe option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unfamiliar sender | Whether anyone on your team requested the share | Check through a known team channel |
| Unexpected sign-in prompt | Whether the file exists in your usual workspace | Open the approved app directly |
| Urgent request for credentials | Whether the request matches company procedure | Report it to your security team |

## Report suspicious messages

1. Leave unexpected links and attachments unopened.
2. Use your organization's official reporting feature or security contact.
3. Include the time, sender, and whether you had requested the file.

### If you already interacted

Tell security promptly if you opened a file, entered information, or approved a prompt. Include the approximate time and what you did. Keep passwords and verification codes out of the report.
