---
title: Diagnose missing calendar invitations
updated: 2026-11-27
category: Software
summary: Trace a missing meeting invitation from the organizer to the attendee's calendar.
---

## Confirm the invitation was sent

Ask the organizer to confirm the attendee's work address and the meeting's date and time zone. If they used a mailing list, ask them to verify that the attendee was a member when the invitation was sent. An address in a forwarded copy may differ from the address on the original invitation.

### Compare the web and desktop calendar

1. Open your approved web calendar and search for the meeting title.
2. Check the same date in the desktop app and confirm both apps are displaying the same account.
3. Look for the organizer's message in the inbox, junk folder, and any folder managed by a mail rule.

## Use the symptom to narrow it down

| Symptom | What to check | Who can help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting exists on the web only | Check whether the desktop app is offline or has stale calendar data | Attendee |
| Other attendees received it | Verify the original recipient address | Organizer |
| Multiple attendees missed it | Check if the organizer received a delivery notice | Service desk |

## Prepare a support ticket

Include the meeting date, approximate send time, and which clients show the event. Use a redacted meeting title if it contains confidential information.
