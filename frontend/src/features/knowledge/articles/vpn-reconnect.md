## Check the connection

1. Open a familiar public site to confirm you have internet access.
2. Disconnect and reconnect in the approved VPN client. Complete any sign-in prompts you initiated.
3. Try opening the internal application again. Check whether another internal application is affected too.

## When to report an incident

If the connection fails again, report the error, the approximate time, and whether you were on home Wi-Fi, office Wi-Fi, or a mobile hotspot. Mention if the problem started after a network change or after your device woke from sleep.

Keep passwords, verification codes, and recovery codes out of the report.
